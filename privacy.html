Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

Halsion keeps your household records on your phone. It needs no account (only if you choose to share a service with your provider), it doesn't track you across apps or websites, and it never sells any data. The free version shows ads.

Your data

Households, services, prices, payments and daily records you enter are stored only on your device, except for services you choose to share (below). Deleting the app, or Settings › Reset All Data, removes them from your phone.

Sharing a service (optional)

If you sign in (with Google, or with Apple on iPhone) and connect a service with your provider or customer, Halsion stores on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) what both phones need to show the same bill: that service's days (date, status, quantity, price, note, and who marked it and when), its prices and settings, the payments recorded for it (amount, dates and note), and both of your display names. Your account's name, email and a user ID are used for signing in. Other services and households stay on your phone. Only you and the person you connected with can see this shared data. Disconnecting stops sharing; Settings › Sharing Account › Delete Account removes your shared data and your account.

Usage, crash and performance reports

To find bugs and improve the app, Halsion sends anonymous reports to Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring): which screens are used, crash details, app speed, and device and app information with a random app installation ID. These reports never include your households, services, prices, records or backups, and they aren't used for advertising or linked to your identity. Google processes them under the Firebase terms and privacy policy.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad in Home, Calendar and Bills. It also offers a short video ad, only when you choose to watch one, for example to add a 3rd service or to share a PDF or CSV statement. Both are served by Google AdMob. Ads are not personalised, and Halsion doesn't share your records with Google. AdMob receives device and app information and your approximate location (from your IP address) to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK you're asked for consent first and can change it in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices. Halsion Premium removes ads.

Backups

Backups are files you choose to save, for example to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Auto Backup saves files on your phone only. Halsion never uploads them anywhere.

Reminders and App Lock

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. App Lock uses your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock through the operating system; Halsion never sees or stores your biometric data.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens a Google Form in your browser. Anything you type there is sent to Google Forms, under Google's privacy policy, only if you choose to submit it.

Children

Halsion is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us through the feedback form on the Support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and included in an app update.
