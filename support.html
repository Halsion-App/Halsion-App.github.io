Frequently asked questions

How do I mark a day?

Open Home or Calendar and tap Present or Absent for each service. Salaried services (maid, cook) also have Half-day and Holiday, a paid day off; daily-paid ones have Half-day. Tap the selected status again to clear it. For milk, pick a quantity; 0 means nothing was delivered.

How is a fixed salary calculated?

A full month is exactly the salary. If a service starts, ends or changes salary mid-month, each part is paid for the working days it covers. Holidays are paid; unmarked days are never deducted.

What happens when I change a price?

Add the new price with the date it starts. It applies from that date onward; earlier days keep the old price. Price History shows each price's dates.

How do payments and balances work?

Open a service in Bills and tap Record Payment. If you pay less than the bill, the rest is carried to next month as due; if you pay more, the extra is carried as an advance.

How do I keep my data safe or move to a new phone?

Use Settings › Backup & Restore to export a backup and save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files. On the new phone, use Restore from Backup. Your data is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it too.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Reminders are sent only on days with something still unmarked. Check Settings › Reminders and that notifications are allowed for Halsion. On some Android phones, also allow Halsion to start automatically and don't restrict its battery use.

What's free, and what does Premium add?

Tracking, bills, payments, reminders and backups are free, with a small banner ad. Halsion Premium is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and adds more households, unlimited services, PDF and CSV statements any time, Auto Backup and App Lock. On a new phone, use Settings › Restore Purchases.

How do I share a service with my provider or customer?

Both of you sign in and add your name. One of you opens the service and taps Show a Code; the other taps Connect with a Code and enters it within 10 minutes. After that, days, prices, settings and payments either of you change show on both phones.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Sharing Account › Delete Account removes your account and your shared data. Everything else is only on your phone; deleting the app removes it.
